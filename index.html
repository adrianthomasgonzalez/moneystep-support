Privacy Policy

How We Handle Your Data

1. Data We Collect

When you create an account and use Moneystep, we collect the following information:

  • Account information: username, email address, phone number, and profile photo
  • Financial information: transaction history, entry fees paid, winnings received, and wallet balance
  • Step count data: daily step totals sourced from Apple HealthKit or Google Health Connect
  • Location data: your general location at the time of account creation for eligibility verification purposes only; this data is not stored beyond the verification check
  • Device information: device token for push notifications, operating system, and app version
  • Usage data: challenge participation history, competition results, and in-app activity

2. Health & Step Data

Moneystep accesses your step count data from Apple HealthKit (iOS) or Google Health Connect (Android) for the sole purpose of tracking your performance in challenges.

Step data retention: We store your step data for up to 90 days to support challenges of varying lengths. Step data older than 90 days is automatically deleted from our servers on a rolling basis. We access historical step data from your health app to calculate your rank tier but do not store it beyond this window.

We do not use your health data for advertising, sell it to third parties, or share it with any entity outside of Moneystep LLC for any purpose other than operating the app.

You may revoke HealthKit or Health Connect permissions at any time through your device settings. Revoking access will prevent step tracking from functioning and may affect your ability to participate in challenges.

3. Third-Party Services

Moneystep uses the following third-party services to operate the platform:

  • Google Firebase — backend infrastructure, database, authentication, and push notifications. Data is stored on Firebase servers subject to Google's privacy policies.
  • Apple HealthKit / Google Health Connect — read-only access to step count data on your device.

We do not integrate advertising networks, analytics SDKs that track behavior across apps, or any other third-party data brokers.

4. Data Sharing & Sale

Moneystep LLC does not sell, rent, or trade your personal data to any third party under any circumstances.

We may disclose information only in the following limited circumstances, including but not limited to:

  • When required by law, court order, or valid legal process
  • To comply with tax reporting obligations, including IRS requirements for issuing Form 1099
  • To prevent fraud, protect the safety of users, or enforce these terms
  • In connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of all or substantially all assets of Moneystep LLC, in which case users will be notified

5. Data Security

We use industry-standard security practices including encrypted data transmission (TLS) and Firebase security rules restricting data access to authenticated users. No method of electronic storage is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security but are committed to protecting your information.

6. Data Retention & Deletion

You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time through the app's account settings or by contacting us at hello@moneystep.app. We will process deletion requests within 30 days. Note that certain financial records may be retained as required by law, including but not limited to tax reporting obligations.

7. Children's Privacy

Moneystep is strictly for users aged 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18. If we become aware that a minor has created an account, we will terminate the account and delete all associated data immediately.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify users of material changes via push notification or email. Continued use of the app after changes take effect constitutes acceptance of the updated policy. The effective date at the top of this page will reflect the most recent revision.

How payment works: When you enroll in a paid challenge, you will be shown payment instructions. Entry fees are collected manually via PayPal or Zelle prior to the challenge start. 5. Exiting a Challenge

You may voluntarily exit a challenge at any time before or after it begins. The following rules apply:

  • Before the challenge starts: You will be removed from the challenge. If you have already submitted payment, refunds are not guaranteed and are issued at Moneystep LLC's sole discretion. You will be free to join another challenge immediately.
  • After the challenge starts: Your entry fee is non-refundable under any circumstances. You will be removed from the challenge and leaderboard. You are free to join another challenge immediately after exiting.

Exits are recorded in your career statistics. Exiting mid-challenge counts as a last-place finish for statistical purposes.

6. Step Data Sync Requirements

To ensure accurate leaderboard standings and final results, users are responsible for the following:

  • Daily sync during a challenge. Open the Moneystep app at least once per calendar day during an active challenge. Step data is synchronized from Apple Health or Google Health Connect when the app is opened or running in the background.
  • Post-challenge sync window. After a challenge ends, open the Moneystep app within 24 hours of the challenge end time to ensure your complete step data is captured before results are finalized.
  • Settlement timing. Challenge results are finalized approximately 24 hours after the challenge end time. Step data not synced by settlement will not be included in your final result.
  • Incomplete sync liability. If you fail to open the app within the 24-hour post-challenge window, your recorded step count at the time of settlement will be used as your final total. Moneystep is not responsible for any difference between your actual steps and your synced step count.
  • Background sync. Moneystep uses background app refresh to automatically sync steps when a challenge ends. If you have disabled background app refresh, or if your device is off or lacks connectivity, you must manually open the app within the window above.
  • Android battery optimization. Some Android device manufacturers (including but not limited to Samsung, Xiaomi, Huawei, and OnePlus) apply aggressive battery saving features that may prevent Moneystep from receiving background sync notifications. If you use one of these devices, we recommend exempting Moneystep from battery optimization in your device settings to ensure reliable step syncing. You can typically find this setting under Battery → App optimization or Battery → Background usage limits. Moneystep is not responsible for missed syncs or incomplete step data resulting from manufacturer battery optimization settings.
  • No liability for device or connectivity issues. Moneystep is not responsible for incomplete, missing, or delayed step data resulting from device settings, device failures, manufacturer restrictions, network outages, or failure to open the app within required timeframes.
